Shalina Prakash

I'm currently a Special Projects Fellow at Secure AI Futures Lab, working on technical AI governance and the economics of TAI. Before this, I was an AI Policy Fellow at Georgia Tech, where I worked on labour shocks in developing economies, and a Research Fellow at Sentient Futures, where I worked on AI's implications for foundational questions in ethics, philosophy of mind and political philosophy. Recently, I graduated from the University of St Andrews, where I read Philosophy. Whilst at university, I interned with the UNDP in India, followed by the GICJ in Geneva and a very brief spell at the Herald in Scotland. Some more research questions I mull over from time to time and eventually want to get to can be found here.
